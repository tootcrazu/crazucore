About Music

So I collect and maintain a custom library of mp3/flac files! It mostly came from a desire to distance from paid subscription services, especially when any piece of music could pretty much disappear at any given time. Plus, it allows me to add anything I can get my hands on, which is great when you want to listen to, for example, pretty much any song from a Nintendo game without going to youtube.


It's actually been quite fun, and something I'd reccomend anyone give a shot if they have the time, the will, and the energy. Meticulously tagging mp3 files and getting them to display properly on different mp3 playing software can be a meticulous task, but it's one that brings me great joy.


If you're interested in what my favorite albums, songs, etc are from my collection, Check them out under the "My Favorites" page!


Someday, I'd like to have a radio of sorts on this website, for easy listening and such. The music player to the right is a sort of prototype of this. Though I'd have to bug Micky P to code in the features that I want to make it fully functional as a radio, and even then It probably woudln't be a perfect solution. Maybe I'll try to learn Java one day!
