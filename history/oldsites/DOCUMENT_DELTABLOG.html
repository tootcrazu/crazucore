Welcome to the super secret not at all secret DELTARUNE chapters 3 & 4 liveblog, which is mostly serving as a place for me to ramble on and on about the chapters, since I don't have people to do that with in regular space. It'll also be fun to look back on, I hope.

We still have 3 Chapters past this point, as crazy as that is to think about.

08:47, June 4th

Only 7 hours and 22 minutes remain. I suppose I should write about thoughts and theories based on what little we know about the chapters.

Right now, it seems obvious that Ch3's dark world (the one created by Kris) will be themed looseley around the TV (thanks to the existence of the Greenroom & everything Tenna related on the sweepstakes, and the TV static from the end of Ch2 and the recent trailer). We also know it will likely incorporate other elements from around the Dreemur residence (The ShadowGuys possibly being reference to the cookie cutters on the windowsil, the 'pizza scent' item reference in one of the newsletter's notes on translation), so I'm interested to see how much of each ends up appearing. After all, the two (three counting Castle Town) dark worlds we've seen have incorporated many elements from all around the room, not just one thing (though I'm sure there would be enough ideas in a TV dark world to fill an entire chapter alone.)

We're also expecting (mostly from guesswork until very recently, with the trailer footage & windows from the new Silence Sweepstakes pages) that Ch4 will feature a dark world located in the church. We've seen gameplay of Kris and Susie there, along with some footage that appears to show them along with Ralsei fighting a relatively Church-y enemy (though I have my suspicions that this footage is altered in some way. It was done to hide some things in Ch2, like Rouxls in the key items inventory).

That was a lot of words to explain mostly what we already know, so I'll do some wishing/speculating now.

  • Hope #1: More new characters in the party!
    Super excited to see what/if new characters will show up! Seeing how each party member ACTs differently in battle and has different equip quotes is one of my favorite parts of the game so far.
    A few new characters that I think have the possibility of showing up, in order of most to least likely: Berdy, Toriel, Catti, Jockington, maaaybe Dess or Asriel (but I would definitely not expect either of those two this early on)


  • Hope #2: Spooky / Serious story?
    A fair bit of the background lore in the game (Disapperance of Dess, person trapped in the code, kris & the player, characters possibly going through therapy, and snowgrave obv) is setting up to be of a more serious tone than the majority of the game has been thus far. I'm looking forward to seeing that evolve. I'd expect this to happen most in Ch4, what with the church being all dark and spooky like.

While all of the Tenna stuff was going on, I (through Kris) travelled to a backroom to play the 'original' (non-tenna-ized) versions of Tenna's games. This was likely Dragon Blazers. In the first, the Dragon Blazers Kris (db.Kris for short) picks up a sword, and defeats various enemies to become stronger. An 'Ice Key' is gained.

In the second, we travel to a place at the north of the map, referred to in text as "THE NORTHERN LIGHT". The door has a Delta Rune sigil on it. Inside the dungeon, you are instructed to 'Follow the White Cloak'. We meet a white robed figure that we can get to follow us around, who has the ability to freeze enemies, which we can use to solve puzzles. Eventually, we are led to a maze of doors, the white cloaked figure refusing to travel down incorrect paths. At the end, we're met with a red Delta Rune door, where we can find the Shelter Key.

The third and final game puts db.Kris in an empty city, with db. versions of Ralsei and Susie in your party. Once you find the sword, you can kill the db. Ralsei and Susie in your party to level up, and proceed further (On replaying this to check, I just noticed that real Kris winces when you do this. I'm so sorry....). Here you meet (a version of?) Tenna, who confesses that a deal was made with Kris. He also discusses talking to Spamton.
z db.Kris can then travel to a version of the shelter inside of the game. Notably, real Kris will attempt to resist moving db.Kris into the shelter doors.
Once inside, we meet what I assume to be a version of The Roaring Knight aka Dess. She says:
"Kris... Oh... Kris..."
"Is it fun, Kris?"
"Playing around like this?"
(I skipped some dialouge, but she tells Kris not to make such a sour expression, since she can see their face in the dark.)
"The Shadow Crystals, and the Shadow Mantle that I'm holding"
"Do you Honestly Think they'll get you what you want?"
Once you defeat her, She will taunt you, saying you can't reach the shadow mantle, even if you wanted it. You can force db.Kris out of the game screen, and put real Kris in, who can reach it. Then Susie comes to give you a soda.

Then, I assume, the Tenna fight continues as normal, though the area is darker and colder. After Tenna is defeated, he is killed from behind by Dess, who promptly atemptes to kidnap the still sleeping Toriel. A battle is engaged with Dess. All party members (except those wearing the Shadow Mantle?) will be K.O'd instantly.

Dess resumes kidnapping Toriel, but is stopped when Undyne drops in and throws some spears (this is when we fully enter territory not even a fanchapter would be bold enough to go down). Dess then kidnapps Undyne and drags her away. Kris and Susie follow, and are led to a door identical to the Great Door that stood between Castle Town and Card Castle. On entering, Kris and Susie are dumped outside of the Dreemur residence, and follow Dess down towards the Shelter. The doors to the shelter are locked, and Susie, frustratedly, gives up hope on trying to rescue Undyne inside. She leaves upwards. Kris stays, where they examine the panel.
